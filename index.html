FAQ

Is the 90/180 count free?

Yes. Trips, map, remaining days and history are free. Planner and PDF are Pro.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-in. Dates, countries and notes live on this iPhone. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Is this legal advice?

No. Roamspan is a planning tool. Border authorities always have the final decision. Short-stay visas, residence permits and some bilateral rules follow other limits.
