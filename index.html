FAQ

Is it free?

Yes for the 90/180 count, unlimited trips, map and history. Planner and PDF are Pro.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-in. Your trips stay on the iPhone.

Is this legal advice?

No. Roamspan is a planning tool. Border authorities always have the final decision. Short-stay visas, residence permits and some bilateral rules follow other limits.
