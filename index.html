FAQ

Is the 90/180 count free?

Yes. Trips, the live map, days remaining and your history are free forever. The planner and PDF export are Pro.

Do I need an account?

No. There is nothing to sign up for. Your dates, countries and notes live on your iPhone — uninstalling the app deletes them.

Is this legal advice?

No. Roamspan is a planning tool, and border authorities always have the final decision. Short-stay visas, residence permits and some bilateral rules follow other limits.

Android?

Not in V1 — Roamspan is currently iPhone-only.
