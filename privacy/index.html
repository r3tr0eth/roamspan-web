Privacy

Your trips (dates, countries, notes) stay on this iPhone. No account is required.

If online, we send anonymous usage stats (screens visited, purchases, product events) to PostHog in the EU, and crash reports to Sentry so we can fix the app. It never includes your itinerary (dates, countries, notes).

Purchases are handled by Apple. You can delete trips in History; uninstalling the app deletes this device’s trips.

Roamspan is a planning tool and is not legal advice or a stay authorisation. Border authorities always have the final decision.
